Our story

A focused platform for metallic yarn and zari sourcing.

Suvarnatantu was created to build a focused B2B platform for metallic yarn and zari requirements. The brand brings together textile knowledge, product development and manufacturing-oriented thinking under Vastranand Pvt. Ltd.

Our purpose is to help buyers approach metallic yarn with clearer product understanding, more useful technical context and direct business communication—from the first application discussion through sampling and RFQ review. Explore the Knowledge Centre for supporting technical context.

Why Suvarnatantu exists

Make metallic-yarn sourcing more structured.

B2B yarn sourcing can become difficult when specifications are unclear, constructions are hard to compare, technical information is limited or the application context is missing. Buyers need a practical way to identify the right yarn direction before moving to samples or commercial discussion.

Suvarnatantu is designed to make that process more transparent and application-focused. We connect the intended textile result with yarn type, construction, denier, TPM, twist, colour and the information available in the buyer brief.

Manufacturing at the core

Product conversations grounded in the textile requirement.

Our product and manufacturing identity centres on metallic yarn, zari and application-specific development. Buyers can explore M Type, MX Type, ST Type, MH Type and custom metallic yarn requirements.

Denier, TPM, twist, colour, finish and construction are considered in relation to the intended textile application. Visit Manufacturing to see the broader requirement-to-supply pathway.
