What is Zari Yarn?

Zari yarn is a decorative metallic yarn used in Indian textiles, including sarees, brocade and embroidery. Its selection depends on the fabric, process, required appearance, colour or finish direction and the technical information available for the requirement.

The five product families below give buyers a practical starting point before sampling or quotation.
