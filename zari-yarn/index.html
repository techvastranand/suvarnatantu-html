What is zari yarn?

Zari yarn is a product category used where a textile brief calls for a metallic or decorative visual direction. The useful starting point is the application, required appearance, colour or finish direction and the technical information already available to the buyer.

For saree and other textile contexts, begin with saree applications, then use Zari Lab to prepare a clearer discussion.

Available categories

Weaving Zari, Embroidery Zari, Imitation Zari, Polyester Zari and Coloured Zari.

Prepare a useful requirement

Share whatever information is available: application, category, colour or finish, known construction, sample/reference and quantity. Not every field is required before starting a discussion.
