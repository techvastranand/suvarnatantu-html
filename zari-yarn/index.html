What is Zari Yarn?

Zari yarn is a product category used when the textile brief calls for a metallic or decorative visual direction. The useful B2B starting point is the application, required appearance, colour or finish direction and the technical information already available to the buyer.

Suvarnatantu treats yarn selection as a connected process: application requirement, category selection, colour and finish discussion, sampling, quality review, approval, production and supply planning.
