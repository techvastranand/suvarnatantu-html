What is Imitation Zari?

Imitation Zari gives sourcing and development teams a clear category reference for a zari-yarn enquiry. It is best reviewed through the end use, required surface effect, colour or finish direction and the specifications already available to the buyer.

The repository does not define exact composition, denier, twist, foil thickness or performance values for this category. Specifications can be developed according to yarn construction, application and buyer requirement.

Sampling and approval by requirement

Sample review and quality validation are considered against the agreed buyer brief. The relevant testing or visual-approval checkpoints are confirmed with the requirement, rather than represented as a universal claim.
