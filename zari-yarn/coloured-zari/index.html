What is Coloured Zari?

Coloured Zari is a category for enquiries in which shade and visual direction are central to the textile brief. The relevant discussion starts with the intended fabric, application, desired appearance and the available buyer context.

Colours provides a starting point for gold, silver, copper, rose gold, antique and custom directions. A colour direction is then reviewed with the application rather than treated as a standalone selection.

Application-driven colour selection

The useful colour discussion links the desired shade to the textile application and required surface result. Sample review, visual approval and quality validation can then be agreed against the buyer brief; no fixed colour-performance or testing claim is made on this page.

Where construction information is needed, specifications can be developed according to yarn construction, application and buyer requirement.
