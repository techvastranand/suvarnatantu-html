What is Weaving Zari?

Weaving Zari is a category for enquiries where the yarn must be considered in relation to the fabric and weave. Existing site application context includes sarees, jacquard, brocade and decorative textiles; the right starting point is the stated textile use and visual objective.

Selection can consider the required appearance, colour or finish direction, fabric context and any known yarn information. Exact construction, machine settings and performance values are confirmed against the buyer requirement rather than assumed.
