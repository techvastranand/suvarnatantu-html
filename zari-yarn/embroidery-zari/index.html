What is Embroidery Zari?

Embroidery Zari is a product category for discussing zari yarn in relation to decorative embroidery applications. The useful brief identifies the textile use, intended visual effect, colour or finish direction and the information already available to the buyer.

This page does not claim compatibility with a particular machine, fixed construction or performance value. Specifications can be developed according to yarn construction, application and buyer requirement.
