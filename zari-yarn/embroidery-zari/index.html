What is Embroidery Zari?

Embroidery Zari Yarn is a zari-yarn category for discussing decorative embroidery requirements in relation to the intended textile use and visual effect. Buyers can share the required appearance, colour or finish direction and any available yarn or sample information so the relevant selection and specification discussion can be reviewed against the application.

This page does not claim compatibility with a particular machine, fixed construction or performance value. Specifications can be developed according to yarn construction, application and buyer requirement.
