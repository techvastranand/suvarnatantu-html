International buyer FAQ

Prepare a clearer export enquiry.

How can an overseas buyer send an enquiry?

Use the International RFQ route with the product, application, destination market and requirement information available.

Can I request a sample before discussing a bulk requirement?

Yes. Use the sample route with the relevant product and application information for review.

What technical details should I provide?

Include available construction, denier, TPM, twist, colour or finish direction and intended application details.

Can I send an existing yarn or sample reference?

Yes. A reference can help make the requirement and sample review more specific.

What commercial details are useful for an international RFQ?

Estimated quantity, sample requirement, destination, packing expectation, shipping responsibility and documentation requirement can help frame the review.

How are shipping, packing and documentation requirements handled?

These requirements are reviewed against the confirmed enquiry, destination and commercial arrangement.

Where can I review company and manufacturing information?

Use the buyer verification links for company, contact, manufacturing, factory, quality, packaging, sample and quote information.
