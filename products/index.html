A quick comparison of our metallic-yarn construction families. Use this as a starting point; final construction and specification should be confirmed through technical review.

Verified qualitative comparison of Suvarnatantu M Type, MX Type, ST Type and MH Type metallic-yarn construction families.
Comparison area M TypeView Details → MX TypeView Details → ST TypeView Details → MH TypeView Details →
Construction Flat metallic-film yarn; the film itself is the basic construction. M-Type metallic film with two supporting filament ends. A textile core covered with metallic flat film. Metallic flat film twisted with a supporting textile filament.
Support / core No support-filament structure in basic M Type. Two fine filament ends; polyester or nylon are listed support options. A textile-filament core; listed options include polyester, rayon / viscose or nylon. A supporting textile filament; listed options include polyester, nylon or rayon.
How components combine Used directly or as the metallic component in other constructions. Support filaments may use balanced clockwise and counter-clockwise directions where specified. Metallic flat film is wrapped around the core yarn. Metallic flat film and the support filament are combined through twisting.
