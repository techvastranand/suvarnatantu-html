For weaving mills, saree manufacturers, embroidery units and sourcing teams, colour selection begins with the intended fabric and visual result. The useful brief considers the application, base yarn or zari category, desired colour or finish, reference context and sample requirement.

Sample review and shade approval are discussed against the buyer requirement. No shade code, coating chemistry, colourfastness value or matching tolerance is assumed on this page.
