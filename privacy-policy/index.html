Privacy Policy

How Suvarnatantu handles information submitted through this B2B website.

Introduction

Suvarnatantu is operated by Vastranand Private Limited. This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through this website is handled in connection with B2B enquiries, quotation requests and sample requests.

Information We Collect

Depending on the form used, we may collect contact and business information such as a contact person’s name, company name, business email address, phone number, country, state or city. We may also collect product, application, colour, technical specification, quantity, delivery, address, tax identifier and other enquiry information that you choose to provide.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, evaluate product or sample requirements, prepare or discuss quotations, communicate about B2B requirements, improve website and enquiry processes, and maintain legitimate business records where necessary.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with internal personnel or necessary service providers only where needed to operate the website, respond to an enquiry or comply with applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant business enquiry, legal obligations or legitimate business records.

Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect submitted information. No internet transmission or system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Cookies and Browser Storage

This website uses browser session storage to carry a product configuration to a related enquiry form during a visit. We do not use that storage for analytics.

Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of information, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements.

Third-Party Links

This website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when needed. Last updated: 9 September 2026.
