Requirement checklist

What should you share for a decorative textile enquiry?

A full technical specification is helpful, but a requirement discussion may begin from partial information and a suitable material or design reference.

  1. 01

    Textile / End Product

    What fabric, textile, trim, product or decorative item is being developed?

  2. 02

    Design Type

    Motif, border, stripe, panel, repeating pattern, all-over or another design type.

  3. 03

    Metallic Area

    Where should the metallic effect appear?

  4. 04

    Desired Colour

    Gold, antique gold, silver, copper, custom/reference, etc.

  5. 05

    Visual Direction

    Prominent, subtle, traditional, contemporary, decorative or reference-led.

  6. 06

    Existing Yarn Details

    If known: construction/type, denier, twist, TPM or other technical information.

  7. 07

    Textile Construction / Process

    If known, describe the relevant weaving, knitting, embroidery, lace, trim, decorative or other process. Compatibility is not inferred.

  8. 08

    Machine / Equipment

    Include only if the buyer already knows the relevant equipment information. Compatibility is not assumed.

  9. 09

    Base Material / Fabric Context

    If known, include information about the other textile materials involved.

  10. 10

    Existing Reference

    Yarn sample, textile sample, finished product, artwork, photograph, colour reference or technical sheet.

  11. 11

    Approximate Quantity

    Development or bulk requirement.

  12. 12

    Special Requirements

    State any technical, visual, dimensional, performance, compliance or processing requirements for separate review.

Technical requirements

Does your application have special technical requirements?

If your decorative textile requires specific machine, processing, dimensional, durability, wash, abrasion, colour, safety, compliance or other technical conditions, include them in the enquiry so they can be reviewed separately.

Do not assume an available yarn construction meets a specific performance or compliance requirement without application-specific review and verification.

Decorative textiles FAQ

Questions for preparing a decorative textile enquiry.

When should I use the Decorative Textiles application route?

Use this route when your metallic or zari yarn requirement does not fit neatly into a more specific application such as saree, jacquard, brocade, embroidery, knitting, lace, weaving or home furnishing.

Do I need to know the exact yarn construction before contacting Suvarnatantu?

No. You can begin with the textile or product, design area, metallic effect, colour direction and available material reference.

What information should I provide?

Share the textile or end product, design type, metallic area, colour direction, desired appearance, known yarn or process information, available reference and approximate quantity.

Can I start from an existing textile, product or yarn sample?

Yes. An existing yarn, textile, finished product, artwork, photograph, shade or technical reference can help begin the requirement discussion.

What if I do not know denier or TPM?

Provide the information you already have. Technical variables can be discussed after the application and reference requirement are clearer.

Can you confirm compatibility with my machine or process?

Machine and process compatibility should be reviewed against the specific textile application, yarn construction and operating requirement. Share the available machine or process details during technical discussion.

Can I request a sample before discussing bulk quantity?

Use the Sample Request route to begin sample discussion, then continue toward RFQ once the requirement is better defined.
