Requirement checklist

What should you share with us?

Complete information is helpful, but buyers may begin with partial information.

  1. 01

    Saree type or fabric

    What kind of saree or fabric are you developing?

  2. 02

    Design area

    Border, pallu, butta, motif, jacquard area, stripe or all-over.

  3. 03

    Desired colour

    Gold, silver, antique, copper, custom or reference.

  4. 04

    Desired visual effect

    Bright, subtle, traditional, contemporary or reference-based.

  5. 05

    Existing yarn details

    If known: construction/type, denier, twist, TPM or other specification.

  6. 06

    Machine / process

    Include this information if it is already known.

  7. 07

    Existing reference

    Yarn sample, saree sample, fabric swatch, photo or technical sheet.

  8. 08

    Required quantity

    Approximate development or bulk requirement.

Saree application FAQ

Questions for preparing a saree yarn enquiry.

Do I need to know the exact yarn type before contacting Suvarnatantu?

No. Begin with the saree application, visual direction and available reference. Add technical yarn details if already known.

What information should I provide for a saree yarn enquiry?

Share the saree or fabric type, design area, colour direction, desired visual effect, known yarn details, reference material and approximate quantity.

Can I send an existing yarn or saree reference?

Yes, the enquiry can begin from an existing yarn, fabric, saree, photograph or technical reference, subject to the available sample or enquiry workflow on the website.

Can I request a sample before discussing bulk quantity?

Use the Sample Request route to begin the sample discussion, then continue toward a bulk RFQ once the requirement is clearer.

What if I do not know denier or TPM?

Provide the information you do know. Technical variables can be discussed once the application and reference requirement are clearer.
