Requirement checklist

What should you share for a lace enquiry?

A complete technical specification is helpful, but an enquiry may begin from partial information and a suitable lace or yarn reference.

  1. 01

    Lace / End Product

    What lace, trim, decorative textile or finished product is being developed?

  2. 02

    Pattern Type

    Floral, geometric, motif, border, repeat, edge or other decorative pattern.

  3. 03

    Metallic Area

    Where should the metallic effect appear in the design?

  4. 04

    Desired Colour

    Gold, antique gold, silver, copper, custom/reference, etc.

  5. 05

    Visual Direction

    Prominent, subtle, traditional, contemporary or reference-led.

  6. 06

    Existing Yarn Details

    If known: construction/type, denier, twist, TPM or other technical information.

  7. 07

    Machine / Process

    Include only if the buyer already knows the relevant production details. Compatibility is not inferred.

  8. 08

    Base Material / Textile

    If known, describe the associated fabric or textile structure.

  9. 09

    Existing Reference

    Yarn sample, lace sample, trim, fabric, artwork, image, shade reference or technical sheet.

  10. 10

    Approximate Quantity

    Development or bulk requirement.

Lace application FAQ

Questions for preparing a lace yarn enquiry.

Do I need to know the exact yarn construction before contacting Suvarnatantu?

No. You can begin with the lace or trim design, metallic effect, colour direction and available yarn or textile reference.

What information should I provide for a lace yarn enquiry?

Share the lace or end product, design type, metallic area, colour direction, desired visual effect, known yarn information, available reference and approximate quantity.

Can I start from an existing lace or yarn sample?

Yes. An existing yarn, lace, trim, fabric, artwork, photograph, shade or technical reference can help begin the requirement discussion.

What if I do not know denier or TPM?

Provide the information you already have. Technical variables can be discussed after the application and reference requirement are clearer.

Do you guarantee compatibility with my lace machine or process?

Machine and process compatibility should be reviewed against the specific application, yarn construction and production requirement. Share the available machine or process details during technical discussion.

Can I request a sample before discussing bulk quantity?

Use the Sample Request route to begin sample discussion, then continue toward RFQ once the requirement is better defined.
