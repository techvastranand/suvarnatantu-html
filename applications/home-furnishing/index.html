Requirement checklist

What should you share for a furnishing enquiry?

A complete specification is helpful, but an enquiry may begin from partial information and a suitable fabric or product reference.

  1. 01

    End Product

    Curtain, cushion textile, panel, table textile, decorative accent, trim or another interior-textile application.

  2. 02

    Fabric / Textile Type

    What textile construction or fabric is being developed, if known?

  3. 03

    Pattern / Design

    Floral, geometric, stripe, motif, border, all-over or other decorative pattern.

  4. 04

    Metallic Area

    Where should the metallic effect appear?

  5. 05

    Desired Colour

    Gold, antique gold, silver, copper, custom/reference, etc.

  6. 06

    Visual Direction

    Prominent, subtle, contemporary, traditional, decorative or reference-led.

  7. 07

    Existing Yarn Details

    If known: construction/type, denier, twist, TPM or other technical information.

  8. 08

    Fabric Construction / Process

    Include only if the buyer already knows the relevant production details. Suitability is not inferred.

  9. 09

    Existing Reference

    Yarn sample, furnishing fabric, finished product, swatch, artwork, interior image, shade reference or technical sheet.

  10. 10

    Approximate Quantity

    Development or bulk requirement.

  11. 11

    Performance Requirements

    State any specific technical requirements so they can be reviewed separately; no requirement is assumed to be met.

Technical requirements

Have specific performance requirements?

If your furnishing textile requires specific wash, abrasion, sunlight, durability, safety, compliance or other performance criteria, include those requirements in the enquiry so they can be reviewed separately.

Do not assume a metallic yarn construction meets a particular performance standard without application-specific review and verification.

Home furnishing FAQ

Questions for preparing a furnishing textile enquiry.

Do I need to know the exact yarn type before contacting Suvarnatantu?

No. You can begin with the furnishing end product, fabric design, metallic effect, colour direction and available material reference.

What information should I provide for a furnishing textile enquiry?

Share the end product, textile or fabric type, pattern, metallic area, colour direction, desired appearance, known yarn details, reference material and approximate quantity.

Can I start from an existing furnishing fabric or product sample?

Yes. An existing yarn, fabric swatch, finished textile, photograph, artwork, shade or technical reference can help begin the requirement discussion.

What if I do not know denier or TPM?

Provide the information you already have. Technical variables can be discussed after the textile application and reference requirement are clearer.

Can you confirm wash, abrasion, sunlight or other performance requirements?

Specific performance requirements should be provided during the technical discussion and reviewed separately against the intended textile application.

Can I request a sample before a bulk enquiry?

Use the Sample Request route to begin sample discussion, then continue toward RFQ once the requirement is better defined.
