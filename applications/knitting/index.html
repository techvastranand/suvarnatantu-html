Requirement checklist

What should you share for a knitting enquiry?

A complete specification is helpful, but an enquiry may begin from partial information and an existing fabric or yarn reference.

  1. 01

    Fabric / End Product

    What knitted textile or finished product is being developed?

  2. 02

    Knitted Structure / Design

    If known, describe the relevant knitted structure, design or visual pattern.

  3. 03

    Metallic Area

    Where should the metallic effect appear?

  4. 04

    Desired Colour

    Gold, antique gold, silver, copper, custom/reference, etc.

  5. 05

    Visual Direction

    Prominent, subtle, traditional, contemporary or reference-led.

  6. 06

    Existing Yarn Details

    If known: construction/type, denier, twist, TPM or other technical details.

  7. 07

    Machine / Gauge / Process

    Include only if the buyer already knows these details. Compatibility is not inferred.

  8. 08

    Base Yarn / Material

    If known, describe the textile or yarn context.

  9. 09

    Existing Reference

    Yarn sample, knitted swatch, finished fabric, garment or product image, shade reference or technical sheet.

  10. 10

    Approximate Quantity

    Development or bulk requirement.

Knitting application FAQ

Questions for preparing a knitting yarn enquiry.

Do I need to know the exact yarn construction before contacting Suvarnatantu?

No. You can begin with the knitted textile, design direction, metallic effect, colour and available yarn or fabric reference.

What information should I provide for a knitting yarn enquiry?

Share the textile or end product, design area, metallic effect, colour direction, known yarn information, available fabric or yarn reference and approximate quantity.

Can I start from an existing knitted swatch or yarn sample?

Yes. An existing yarn, knitted fabric, finished textile, photograph, shade reference or technical specification can help begin the requirement discussion.

What if I do not know denier, TPM or gauge?

Provide the information you already have. Technical variables can be discussed after the textile application and reference requirement are clearer.

Do you guarantee compatibility with my knitting machine?

Machine and process compatibility should be reviewed against the specific yarn construction, fabric structure and operating requirement. Share the available machine, gauge or process details during technical discussion.

Can I request a sample before discussing bulk quantity?

Use the Sample Request route to begin sample discussion, then continue toward RFQ once the requirement is better defined.
