Requirement checklist

What should you share for an embroidery enquiry?

A complete technical specification is useful, but an enquiry may begin with partial information and a suitable design or material reference.

  1. 01

    Fabric / End Product

    What textile, garment or finished product is being developed?

  2. 02

    Embroidery Type / Design

    Motif, border, logo, floral, geometric, highlight or all-over design.

  3. 03

    Metallic Area

    Where the metallic effect appears within the embroidered artwork.

  4. 04

    Desired Colour

    Gold, antique gold, silver, copper or custom/reference.

  5. 05

    Visual Direction

    Prominent, subtle, traditional, contemporary or reference-led.

  6. 06

    Existing Yarn Details

    If known: construction/type, denier, twist, TPM or other technical details.

  7. 07

    Embroidery Process / Machine

    Include only if the buyer knows and wishes to provide these details.

  8. 08

    Fabric / Base Material

    Describe the base textile if known.

  9. 09

    Existing Reference

    Yarn sample, embroidered sample, fabric, artwork, photograph, shade reference or technical sheet.

  10. 10

    Approximate Quantity

    Development or bulk requirement.

Embroidery application FAQ

Questions for preparing an embroidery yarn enquiry.

Do I need to know the exact yarn construction before contacting Suvarnatantu?

No. You can begin with the embroidery design, textile, colour direction, metallic effect and available yarn or sample reference.

What information should I provide for an embroidery yarn enquiry?

Share the fabric or end product, design type, metallic area, colour direction, desired visual effect, known yarn information, available reference and approximate quantity.

Can I start from an existing embroidered sample or yarn?

Yes. An existing yarn, embroidered textile, artwork, photograph, shade reference or technical specification can help begin the requirement discussion.

What if I do not know denier or TPM?

Provide the information you already have. Technical variables can be discussed after the application, design and reference requirement are clearer.

Do you guarantee compatibility with my embroidery machine?

Compatibility should be reviewed against the specific application, yarn construction and process requirements. Share the available machine or process information during technical discussion.

Can I request a sample before a bulk RFQ?

Use the Sample Request route to begin the sample discussion, then continue toward RFQ once the requirement is better defined.
