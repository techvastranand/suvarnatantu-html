What is metallic yarn?

Metallic yarn is a textile yarn category used where a fabric requires a metallic or decorative visual direction. Suvarnatantu offers M Type, MX Type, ST Type, MH Type and custom metallic-yarn routes for buyers to frame a product discussion around application, appearance and available technical context.
