Product overview

What is M Type Metallic Yarn?

M Type Metallic Yarn is the flat metallic-film form used as a base metallic component for decorative textile applications. It is commonly produced from slit metallised PET film, which may use an aluminium metallised layer and a protective surface coating to create metallic lustre and provide surface protection.

Unlike MX, ST and MH constructions, basic M Type is the flat metallic film itself rather than a metallic component combined with supporting textile filaments. The suitable film width, thickness, finish and colour direction depend on the textile process, required appearance and available sample or buyer specification.

M Type FAQs

Questions buyers commonly ask

What is M Type Metallic Yarn?

M Type is a flat metallic-film construction commonly produced from slit metallised PET film. It is used directly for decorative textile applications or as a metallic component in other yarn constructions.

How is M Type different from MX Type?

Basic M Type is flat metallic film, while MX Type combines M Type film with two supporting textile filament ends.

What film thicknesses are used for M Type?

Typical industry references include approximately 12μ, 23μ and 25μ. The appropriate thickness should be reviewed against the intended application and required appearance.

Can M Type colour and width be customised?

Colour, width and construction requirements can be discussed with Suvarnatantu, subject to available production and development capability. Use Request a Quote or Request a Sample to share the brief.
