Product overview

What is MH Type Metallic Yarn?

MH Type is commonly made by twisting M-Type metallic flat yarn with a nylon, polyester or rayon supporting filament. The filament selection and final twist are reviewed against the intended fabric and processing route.

Typical industry references include approximately 70D nylon, 68D or 75D polyester, and 75D rayon support yarns. They are reference options only, not fixed Suvarnatantu specifications.

  • Twisted metallic construction
  • Textile-filament support
  • Flexible metallic-textile effect
