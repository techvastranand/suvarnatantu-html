Development starts with the buyer requirement.

A useful custom brief brings together the application, desired appearance, colour direction, known yarn-construction context, end use, quantity context and sample requirement. It lets the team understand what the textile needs before any construction or commercial assumption is made.

Suvarnatantu does not present custom development as a fixed catalogue specification. Feasibility and relevant technical details are reviewed against the complete buyer requirement.

Custom Metallic Yarn FAQs

Plan a productive development conversation.

What should I include in a custom metallic yarn enquiry?

Include the application, desired appearance, colour direction, known construction information, end use, quantity context and whether samples are required.

Can I discuss technical specifications?

Yes. Use Zari Lab and the available brief as a shared reference, then submit the requirement through Request a Quote.

Can samples be requested before production planning?

Yes. Use Request Samples to begin the sample-development discussion.
