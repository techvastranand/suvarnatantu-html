What Does TPM Mean in a Yarn Specification?

TPM means twists per metre. It is a specification term used to discuss twist level in a yarn; the relevant choice should be considered alongside material, construction and intended application.

Use TPM to describe the twist context of a yarn requirement.

TPM means twists per metre and provides a shared way to discuss the amount of twist in a yarn. It is read alongside twist direction, intended textile application, construction information and the product characteristics being considered.

This guide explains the terminology without prescribing a TPM value. The relevant twist context depends on the textile brief and the specification details available for review.
