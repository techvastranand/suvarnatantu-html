S-Twist vs Z-Twist Yarn

S and Z identify yarn twist direction. This terminology helps buyers, production teams and suppliers discuss a requirement precisely alongside TPM, material and intended application.

Twist-direction reference

Use S and Z terminology to describe the yarn construction clearly.

S-twist and Z-twist describe the direction of yarn twist. Identifying the direction alongside TPM, intended textile application and available construction information helps a buyer communicate the technical brief with greater clarity.

This guide provides terminology rather than a product recommendation. The final yarn choice depends on the application and the specification details being reviewed.
