How to Specify Yarn Denier in a Requirement

Use this buyer guide when preparing a yarn requirement that includes denier.

Share the known denier alongside yarn type, construction, intended application, finish and any reference sample. Denier describes one part of the brief, so the surrounding requirement helps place it in the right product context.

What to include with a denier requirement

Include the yarn type, known construction, intended textile application, required finish and sample reference where available. This makes the requirement clearer without treating denier as a universal measure of quality or suitability.
