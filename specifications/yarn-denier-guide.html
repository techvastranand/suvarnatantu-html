Denier reference

Use denier as one part of a complete yarn brief.

Denier is a yarn specification term used to describe linear density. It is most useful when considered alongside the intended textile application, material direction, twist context and the appearance or finish required for the product.

This guide helps buyers use the term accurately when comparing yarn information. It does not prescribe a denier value; the relevant specification depends on the textile brief being reviewed.
