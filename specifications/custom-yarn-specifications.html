Discuss Your Specification

Turn a textile brief into a clearer yarn specification.

Custom yarn specifications begin with the intended textile application and the product details available to the buyer. Denier, TPM, twist direction, colour, finish, packing and product or lot identification can provide a shared basis for reviewing a non-standard requirement.

Not every option is assumed. The useful next step is to identify the information already known and discuss how it relates to the chosen yarn category and intended use.
