Supplier context for saree production

The buying conversation is different from an application guide: it focuses on communicating the required appearance, selected yarn category, colour direction and repeat specification context to a supplier. Weaving Zari and Metallic Yarn give useful category context.

Saree Applications supports end-use discussion, while Colours and Zari Lab help prepare a clearer buyer brief.
