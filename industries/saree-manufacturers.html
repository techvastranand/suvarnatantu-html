Keep approved saree references clear

A reviewed sample gives the saree team a common reference for appearance, product identity and colour. For a repeat design, identify the approved sample or earlier specification and note where the yarn is used in the saree.

Include known construction details when available, along with the design context and quantity. This keeps a repeat requirement tied to the actual saree brief rather than to colour alone.

What should a saree manufacturer include with a sample request?

Describe the saree area or decorative use, desired zari appearance, colour or finish, available yarn details and any fabric, swatch or sample reference.

How should a repeat saree requirement be communicated?

Identify the approved sample or specification, the design area where the yarn is used and any current quantity or colour context for review.
