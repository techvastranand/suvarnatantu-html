What should an importer include in the first enquiry?

Provide the product family, intended application, known specification, colour or finish, sample status, estimated quantity, destination and any packing or documentation requirements already known.

When should packing and documentation be discussed?

Include buyer-specific requirements in the RFQ so they can be reviewed with the product, quantity, destination and proposed commercial arrangement.
