Build an application-led yarn assortment

Start with the downstream buyers the range must support. Products provides the available families, while Applications helps organise the assortment around weaving, saree, embroidery and decorative textile requirements.

Keep each item identified by product family, type or category, colour or finish and sample reference. A clear name gives sales teams and downstream buyers a common starting point for technical questions.

Multiple buyer routes

Use Weaving, Embroidery and Decorative Textiles to frame different customer conversations.

Custom requirements

Record the buyer's application, desired appearance and available technical details before opening a custom yarn discussion.

Give downstream buyers clearer product information

Present samples with enough context for a buyer to ask a useful question: product family, product name, colour or finish and the application route being considered. Add construction, denier, TPM or twist when those details form part of the reference.

Metallic Yarn, Zari Yarn and Technical Data give distributor teams product and terminology references for these conversations.

Buyer presentation samples

Use samples to present identified options and capture which reference a downstream buyer wants reviewed.

Structured commercial enquiry

Combine the selected reference with the application, requested specification, quantity and custom context in the RFQ.

Move from sample reference to repeat enquiry

When a buyer returns to an earlier sample, preserve that identification through the next enquiry. Note the downstream application and any requested change so repeat-order communication remains specific.

Reference continuity

Carry the selected product name, sample identity and finish from buyer presentation into commercial coordination.

Application change

If the same buyer considers another textile use, include the new application and technical context for review.

How can a distributor support different downstream applications?

Organise identified samples by product family and application, then collect the buyer's intended use, appearance and known specification before requesting review.

What should accompany a repeat distributor enquiry?

Provide the earlier sample or product reference, downstream application, current quantity and any change to construction, colour, finish or custom requirement.
