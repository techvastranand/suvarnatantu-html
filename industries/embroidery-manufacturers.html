Define the embroidery effect first

Describe how the metallic yarn should appear in the finished embroidery and identify the textile or product being decorated. Embroidery Zari and Embroidery Applications provide the closest product and end-use context.

When the brief calls for a different construction route, review Metallic Yarn. Use Colours to connect the required shade or finish with the decorative result.

Yarn construction

Share any known construction, denier, twist or sample identity. Zari Lab can help organise the technical language.

Process requirements

Where machine or process requirements are important, include those details so the yarn brief can be reviewed against the intended embroidery use.

Evaluate the sample against the design

Review the proposed yarn for metallic appearance, colour or finish and construction in relation to the embroidery design. An existing yarn, stitched reference or visual brief can help identify what the buyer expects.

After sample review, carry the selected product reference, application, known process information and quantity into the bulk discussion. Commercial terms are reviewed against the final specification and quantity.

What information helps identify an embroidery yarn requirement?

Share the intended embroidery use, decorative effect, colour or finish, known yarn construction and any machine, process or sample information relevant to selection.

Can an existing embroidery reference be reviewed?

Yes. Include the available yarn, stitched sample or visual reference with the application details so the requirement can be discussed more precisely.
