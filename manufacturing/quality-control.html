Before supply

A concise review checklist

The exact review remains specific to the agreed requirement. Buyer-specific testing or documentation needs should be raised during the technical enquiry.

  • Buyer requirement and available reference are clear.
  • Product identity and relevant construction context have been reviewed.
  • Appearance, colour or finish have been considered where relevant.
  • Order context is carried into packing and dispatch preparation.
