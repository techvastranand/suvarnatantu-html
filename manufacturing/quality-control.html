Useful quality discussion can include product identity, yarn construction, visual appearance, colour or finish direction, sample-reference context and order information. These checkpoints support communication between buyer and manufacturing teams without asserting universal pass or fail thresholds.

Exact test methods, equipment, tolerance limits, AQL processes, certification systems and guarantees are not published here without approved business data.

Before dispatch

Final review is discussed against the agreed buyer requirement, product reference and order context before Packaging & Dispatch. A technical enquiry should state the controls or documentation that matter to the buyer.

Can samples be reviewed before production?

Yes. Request a sample with the intended application and available reference so the next review can be discussed in context.

What should be included in a quality enquiry?

Provide the product identity, application, relevant specification terms, appearance or colour direction and any sample-reference context already available.
