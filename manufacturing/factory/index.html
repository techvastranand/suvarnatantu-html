Requirement-led manufacturing

A controlled path from buyer brief to supply planning

The working reference starts with the product category, intended textile application and information supplied by the buyer. Construction, denier, TPM, twist, colour or finish and sample context can be considered where they are relevant to the enquiry.

Sampling and buyer approval establish the reference for production coordination. The same requirement context then carries into quality review, packing preparation and dispatch coordination.

Production path

How the requirement moves forward

Each stage keeps the production conversation connected to the information and reference agreed with the buyer.

  1. 01

    Requirement Review

    Product category, application and available specification information establish the enquiry context.

  2. 02

    Sample / Reference

    A physical sample or clearly described reference gives the review a shared starting point.

  3. 03

    Specification Approval

    The known construction, denier, TPM, twist, colour or finish details are aligned where relevant.

  4. 04

    Production Coordination

    The approved requirement provides the context for planning the production step.

  5. 05

    Production

    The requirement advances through the published production pathway.

  6. 06

    Quality Review

    Product identity, construction, appearance, colour or finish and sample context guide the review.

  7. 07

    Packing Preparation

    Order and product context are carried into preparation for packing.

  8. 08

    Dispatch Coordination

    The order moves into the relevant packing and dispatch conversation.
