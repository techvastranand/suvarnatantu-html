Packaging and dispatch for B2B yarn supply connects the finished product with the information a buyer needs to identify it. Product, lot or batch references, specification labels and packing details can be aligned before dispatch preparation.

This manufacturing-stage process supports clear handover from production and quality review into buyer-facing packing and documentation communication. It does not define export-specific standards or logistics arrangements.
