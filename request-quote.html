Request a Metallic and Zari Yarn Quote

Submit the information available for your product, application and commercial requirement. Technical fields remain optional where they are not yet known.

What helps prepare a useful RFQ

Include the product or yarn type, intended application, colour or finish direction, known construction or technical requirement, sample or reference requirement, estimated quantity, destination market or country, company details and special notes.

Products, Samples, Zari Lab and Manufacturing can help prepare the buyer brief before submission.

Who should request a quote?

Textile manufacturers, sourcing teams, distributors, trade buyers and companies with a B2B yarn requirement.

Next step

The enquiry creates a basis for technical and commercial discussion. No price, minimum order, lead-time or delivery commitment is stated on this page.

RFQ FAQs

What details help prepare a quotation?

Product category, application, colour or finish, technical requirement, sample reference, estimated quantity and company details help create a clearer enquiry.

Can I include a sample or reference requirement?

Yes. Describe the reference in the message and use the Samples route where evaluation is needed.

Can I discuss a custom yarn specification?

Yes. Share the known requirement and use Technical Specifications for relevant terminology.
