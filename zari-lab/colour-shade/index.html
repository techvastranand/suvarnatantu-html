From colour direction to a usable reference

Colour selection begins with the visual direction required for the textile. Shade discussion adds the buyer’s reference, application context and sample review to that direction, helping the requirement be communicated more clearly.

Colour and shade are not technical-performance claims. This page does not claim colour matching systems, tolerance values, instruments, lab-dip processes or colourfastness standards.
