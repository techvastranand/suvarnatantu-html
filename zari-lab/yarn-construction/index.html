Yarn Construction Guide

Yarn construction describes how the components of a yarn are brought together for a product requirement.

Construction is part of the application decision

Construction describes the components in a yarn and the way they are combined. A buyer brief may identify a metallic film or strip, a core or support yarn, and a twist or wrapping relationship.

Construction varies by product requirement. Use the known film, core or support yarn, wrap and sample information to describe the required structure alongside TPM, denier, twist direction, colour and application.
