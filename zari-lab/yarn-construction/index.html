Yarn Construction Guide

Yarn construction describes how the components of a yarn are brought together for a product requirement.

Request a SampleDiscuss a Specification

Construction is part of the application decision

A construction discussion may consider the role of a core, wrap or other components where relevant to the product brief. The useful question is not whether a single construction is universally best, but how the known construction relates to the intended textile appearance, process and application.

Metallic and zari appearance can be discussed alongside construction, TPM, denier, twist direction, colour or finish and sample reference. This page does not assign specific material structures to product categories.

Specification inputs

Denier, TPM and Twist add useful context.

Product categories

Metallic Yarn and Zari Yarn help frame the enquiry.

Custom construction discussion

For a custom requirement, share the application, intended effect, available construction details and any sample or reference. Construction can then be reviewed with the buyer requirement before approval, rather than assumed from an application name alone.
