What yarn twist means

Twist direction describes the orientation of the visible spiral or surface lines when a yarn is viewed vertically. S twist and Z twist are the standard terms used to identify that direction.

Include the required direction with TPM, construction and application when preparing a technical brief. Machine or process information can also be supplied when it affects the buyer requirement.

S Twist and Z Twist Direction

S and Z describe the direction in which the visible yarn twist runs when viewed vertically.

Twist direction describes orientation. TPM describes how much twist is present. The two specification terms should not be treated as the same measurement.

Application and sample review

Include twist direction with the intended application, construction information and any sample reference. This keeps the S or Z orientation connected to the complete yarn requirement.
