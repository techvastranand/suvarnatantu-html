What yarn twist means

Twist describes the directional arrangement introduced into a yarn. S twist and Z twist are standard labels for that direction; the terminology supports precise communication, not a universal product recommendation.

Twist direction is considered with yarn construction, TPM, product category and intended application. A machine-specific recommendation is not assumed on this page.

Application and sample review

When twist direction is relevant to an enquiry, include it with the intended application, construction information and any sample reference. The requirement can then be reviewed rather than inferred from a general rule.

What is S twist and Z twist?

S and Z are labels used to identify yarn twist direction.

Why does twist direction matter?

It gives buyers, production teams and suppliers a consistent way to describe the required yarn construction alongside the application and TPM context.
