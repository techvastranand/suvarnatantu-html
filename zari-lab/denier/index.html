Why denier matters

Denier gives buyers a clearer way to discuss fineness when comparing yarn information. It can be considered with construction, intended appearance and the textile application, rather than as an isolated indicator of strength, quality or suitability.

One denier does not suit every application. The relevant specification depends on the product category, construction, process and buyer requirement being reviewed.

What does denier measure?

Denier is a linear-mass measure used to describe yarn fineness.

Does denier alone determine yarn quality?

No. It is one specification input and should be considered with construction, application and the buyer’s requirement.
